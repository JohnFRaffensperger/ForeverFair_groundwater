Trader guide

Researcher overview

What this system does

Forever Fair runs a groundwater allocation auction. Traders submit bids by period and well. The system clears bids against environmental constraints and returns accepted quantities and implied prices.

Before you participate

  • Your account must be configured by the auction manager.
  • Your wells must be recorded in the system.
  • You should understand market rules and payment obligations.
  • Your metering and reporting obligations must be in place.

How to submit a bid

  1. Log in at the participant login page and select your participant identity.
  2. Open the trader page.
  3. Select a well and auction period.
  4. Enter quantity and price for each tradable period. (Consider prohibiting raising bids for later periods. Warn that future quota won now can be scaled down.)
  5. Submit your bid. It will then appear in your bid history.

Bidding Principle

Bid your value for the next unit of water. In practice this is a stepwise value curve: your first units may be highly valuable, while additional units may be worth less. A future release will support fuller multi-step bid curves directly in the UI.

Trading cadence and flexibility

  • Auctions are expected to run regularly (for example weekly, and more frequently in drought).
  • Tentative auction runs are useful for learning likely outcomes before final commitment.
  • If you do not rebid in a cycle, prior bids can be reused automatically when enabled by the manager.

Current validation rules

  • Auction must be open.
  • The selected well must belong to your participant account.
  • Period must be part of the active auction.
  • Quantity and price must be positive.

Interpreting results

  • Accepted quantity may be less than submitted quantity.
  • Prices are driven by binding control-point constraints.
  • Future periods may be revised in later operational updates.

Planned extension

A future release is planned to allow trader registration without an owned well, so a trader can buy water on behalf of an control point<\p>
