Trader guide

What this system does

Forever Fair runs a groundwater allocation auction. Traders submit bids by period and well. The system clears bids against environmental constraints and returns accepted quantities and implied prices.

Before you participate

  • Your account must be configured by the auction manager.
  • Your wells must be recorded in the system.
  • You should understand market rules and payment obligations.
  • Your metering and reporting obligations must be in place.

We will adjust your initial quota to the available water.

For the first auction, and any auction where the forecasted aquifer head changes, we will scale your quota separately for each control point and time period. We will convert your well quota will be converted to time-based quota for each environmental constraint.

How to submit a bid

Bid your value for the next unit of water. In practice this is a stepwise value curve: your first units may be highly valuable, while additional units may be worth less.

  1. Log in at the login page and select your trader identity.
  2. Use the row for the target auction period.
  3. Enter quantity and price for the first bid step, then add optional later steps in the same row.
  4. Submit the row. It will then appear in your bid history.
  5. Standing bids: We will reuse your last bids unless you change them.

Trading schedule and flexibility

  • Auctions are expected to run regularly (for example weekly, and more frequently in drought).
  • Tentative auction runs are useful for learning likely outcomes before final commitment.
  • If you do not rebid in a cycle, we will automatically re-use your prior bids.

Current validation rules

  • Auction must be open.
  • The selected well must belong to your trader account.
  • Period must be part of the active auction.
  • Quantity and price must be positive.

Interpreting results

  • Accepted quantity may be less than submitted quantity.
  • Prices are driven by binding control-point constraints.
  • Future periods may be revised in later operational updates.
