Researcher overview to Forever Fair

Single entry point to all application pages and documentation.

Forever Fair is a periodic groundwater allocation auction. Each auction covers one or more delivery periods. A catchment manager sets up each auction, opens it for bidding, runs the clearing model, and publishes results. Traders log in, submit or revise bids, and view their accepted quantities and prices. Traders who hold water rights submit bids stating how much water they want to buy or sell in each period, and at what price. The system then clears the market: it solves an optimization model that accepts or rejects bids to maximise the total value of trade while keeping pumping within the environmental limits set by the hydrologist.

Key ideas:

  • Environmental limits first. The hydrologist sets hard bounds on water-level changes at control points around the catchment. These bounds cannot be overridden by high bids.
  • Location-specific prices. Because pumping at one location affects water levels elsewhere, the clearing price can differ from well to well and from period to period.
  • Voluntary trade. Each well has an underlying resource consent (a right to abstract water). Traders can sell water they do not need, or buy extra above their consent if the market allows.
  • Periodic auctions. Auctions run on a regular schedule. A tentative run before closing lets traders see likely outcomes and adjust their bids.
